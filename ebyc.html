Our Story

More Than Boxing

EBYC — Elite Boxing Youth Champs, Inc. — is a 501(c)(3) non-profit born from the belief that every child, regardless of their economic situation, deserves access to quality coaching and mentorship.

Founded alongside Elite Boxing Fitness Center in Oviedo, FL, EBYC bridges the gap for youth who otherwise couldn't afford structured athletic and personal development programs. We use boxing not as an end goal, but as a vehicle for life-changing discipline, education, and community.

Serving youth ages 8–17 in Central Florida
Focused on low-income and at-risk populations
Registered 501(c)(3) — donations are tax-deductible

What We Provide

Beyond the Ring

EBYC delivers a complete program — from technical boxing skills to life skills that equip youth for every challenge they'll face.

Boxing Technique

Proper stance, footwork, combinations, and fundamentals taught by expert coaches with real competitive experience.

Discipline & Respect

Boxing demands structure and self-control. EBYC instills values of respect, accountability, and personal responsibility that last a lifetime.

Education

Sessions include educational topics beyond boxing — bullying prevention, depression awareness, stress management, and resilience training.

Mentorship

Youth are paired with caring adult coaches who provide consistent guidance, support, and positive role models in their lives.

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

EBYC spots are filled on a rolling basis depending on available funding. If you believe your child qualifies, reach out — we want to hear from you.

Requirements

Ages 8–17 years old
Resident of Central Florida
Under-resourced household — low-income or facing economic hardship
At-risk youth facing social, emotional, or community challenges
Availability subject to program funding

What's Included

Subsidized or sponsored class access
Expert coaching from the Elite Boxing staff
Full boxing technique curriculum
Educational workshops and life-skills sessions
Ongoing mentorship and community
